LongMemEval's full MIT license and copyright notice is included in `notices/LongMemEval-MIT.txt`. The PersonaMem-v2 data page displays CC BY 4.0, so an MIT label from another, older version is not applied to this dataset. Saved BEAM judgment reasons are evaluation results, distinct from original rubrics.

The [official PersonaMem-v2 data card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/bowen-upenn/PersonaMem-v2) says its data was generated with LLMs and that sensitive/personal-information-like values were pseudonymized or generated without entering real information. It also notes the possibility of accidental coincidence with real information. Accordingly, contact-like wording retained from this benchmark in this package is not interpreted as a real person's data or an operational credential. This is the source provider's description of PersonaMem-v2, not a general statement about all datasets.

The [official BEAM data card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Mohammadta/BEAM) and [original paper, sections 2.2 and B.3.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.27246v1) describe generating conversations and user profiles/plans to construct synthetic interactions. Place wording in that benchmark's rental/inheritance scenarios is preserved in this generated-data context. Key- or password-like candidates in BEAM answers were placeholders in code examples or ordinary local-database connection examples, and email-shaped candidates came from HTML examples. This classification is neither verification of live credentials nor a guarantee that generated values can never coincide with reality. LongMemEval's phone-number-like candidate occurred in a theme-park product business-contact context and was not verified as a private person's contact.

Published choices for 4,895 PersonaMem questions preserve saved order and string formatting. For 105 other questions, provider-key-like or private-path-like patterns in original benchmark wording prompted omission of question/reference/options, leaving official source links and CSV row positions. These omissions are changes in this release's selection/excerpt, not modifications made by the original authors. They do not establish usable credentials or real private paths. GPT answers and scores remain for all 5,000 PersonaMem questions. LoCoMo and HaluMem are not categorically barred from every public use.
